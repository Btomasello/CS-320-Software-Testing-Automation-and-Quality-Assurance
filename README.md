# CS-320-Portfolio Project (Software Testing, Automation, and Quality Assurance)

*How can I ensure that my code, program, or software is functional and secure?
When it comes to ensuring functionality, there are a few factors to consider. I always have a tendency to test the foundation and structure of code first and foremost. Having a solid base to work from allows for everything else to move properly. It's important to consider checking for bugs and errors throughout development as well. It's essential to always test multiple times to ensure that everything is running as it should.

*How do I interpret user needs and incorporate them into a program?
Considering user needs is a standard part of development. The success of a project starts with knowing what the user wants. Taking their needs into consideration throughout each phase of development will ensure that the requirements are met on both the technical and user side of production. Incorporating these needs can make or break the product as a whole. It's important to understand what it will take for a userbase to give positive feedback.

*How do I approach designing software?
I have a linear process for software design. Taking into consideration what the product needs to entail and what it should look like at the end is where I start first. From here, it's important to have a guideline and structure to follow. The typical development cycle would take place but with a keen interest on user needs. I never want to be in a situation where I have to go back to the drawing board because the client or user is unhappy. It's important that throughout development that all participating parties have clear and open communication. Aside from this, I would also incorporate a decent amount of user research to ensure I am creating the best possible product.
